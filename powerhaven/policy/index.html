Powerhaven Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Powerhaven ("the game") is an action RPG made by Bartłomiej Mamzer, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the Powerhaven game for Android and iOS collects, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: bartlomiejmamzergames@gmail.com.

In short

  • No account, no ads, no ad tracking.
  • No GPS or precise location. If you share statistics, GameAnalytics estimates your country from your IP address.
  • Your age is asked once and stays on your device.
  • Gameplay statistics are sent only if you are old enough to agree and you say yes. They carry a random ID the game makes, never your name or account. You can change your answer at any time in Settings.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store; I never see your payment details.
  • Your progress can be saved to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center so you can continue on another device.

1. Your age

On first launch the game asks how old you are. The answer is stored only on your device, is never sent to me or anyone else, and is used only to choose the right settings: players under 13, or under the age of digital consent in their country (for example 16 in Germany), never send any statistics.

2. Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics)

If you are old enough to agree and you choose SHARE, the game sends gameplay statistics to GameAnalytics ApS, my analytics service, to help me balance and improve the game: for example rifts started, completed or failed, rewards earned and spent, store purchases (product and price, never payment details), game errors, and basic device information (device model, operating system version, game version, language and country).

Statistics are tied to a random identifier the game creates; it is not your name, your Google or Apple account, or an advertising identifier. GameAnalytics processes your IP address to estimate your country (approximate location); the game never asks for GPS or precise location. See GameAnalytics' privacy policy.

If you choose NO THANKS, or turn ANALYTICS off in Settings, nothing is sent and the analytics service is not started.

3. Purchases

Gem packs are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which process the payment under their own privacy policies (Google, Apple). The game receives a transaction number for each purchase and keeps it in your save so a purchase is granted exactly once.

4. Cloud save

If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), your game progress is saved there so you can continue on another device. The save holds your progress (heroes, levels, items, currencies, play time, the generated player name shown in the game, and purchase transaction numbers). It is stored by Google or Apple under their policies; I do not run my own servers.

5. Children

Powerhaven is meant for players aged 13 and over. Players who say they are younger, or below the age of digital consent in their country, can still play, but the game sends no statistics for them.

6. Keeping and deleting data

  • Everything stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • Cloud saves can be deleted in the Google Play Games app (settings, delete data) or through Game Center and iCloud settings on iOS.
  • To delete statistics already sent, email bartlomiejmamzergames@gmail.com with the Statistics ID shown in Settings (tap it to copy it), and I will ask GameAnalytics to erase them.

7. Legal bases and your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland: gameplay statistics are sent only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. Purchases and cloud saves are processed to provide what you asked for.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. For statistics, send your Statistics ID as described above. For data held by Google, Apple or GameAnalytics, you can also contact them directly.

8. Changes to this policy

I will update this page when the game changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: bartlomiejmamzergames@gmail.com.
