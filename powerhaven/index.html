Powerhaven

Quick rifts, epic loot and a haven to build. A bright, fast action RPG for Android and iOS.

Pick your hero, jump into a rift, smash through rooms full of enemies and come home with loot. Every rift is a 2 to 5 minute run with elites, a twist that changes the fight and a guardian or a boss at the end. Between runs, build your haven piece by piece.

No ads

Powerhaven has no ads and plays offline. Gems can be bought in the game, and your progress can be saved to Google Play Games or Game Center.
