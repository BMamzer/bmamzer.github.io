Privacy Policy

Mobile games (Android and iOS)
Effective Date: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile games for Android and iOS that are published on Google Play and the Apple App Store and that link to this page (the "Games"). It explains what information is processed when you play, why, who receives it, and the choices you have.

Not every Game uses every feature described here. For example, a Game without in-app purchases or cloud saves does not process the information described in those sections. The versions of our games played in a web browser (for example on web game portals) are not covered by this policy; they are subject to the privacy policy of the website you play them on.

We do not require you to create an account, we do not run our own servers for the Games, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

1. Your age

When you first start a Game, it asks how old you are. Your answer (and the month you gave it) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. The Game uses it to decide how advertising and analytics must work for you:

  • Children (under 13, or under the age at which you can consent to data processing yourself in your country, which is between 13 and 16 in the EU): ads are requested with child-directed treatment and without the device's advertising identifier, no consent or tracking prompts are shown, and no analytics are collected.
  • Teens (13 to 17): only non-personalized ads are shown, there is no tracking prompt, and analytics are collected only if you are old enough to consent in your country and you agree.
  • Adults (18 and over): advertising and analytics follow the consent choices described below.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, your age answer and your consent choices are stored locally on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the Game (unless you use cloud saves, see section 6).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some Games show ads (for example, optional rewarded videos) through Google AdMob, a service of Google. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may process your IP address, device and app information (such as device model, operating system and language), ad interactions and, where allowed, your device's advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA on iOS).

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ads are requested, the Game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can accept or refuse personalized advertising there, and you can change your choice at any time through the privacy options in the Game's settings.
  • Personalized ads are only shown to adults who allow them. Teens and children only receive non-personalized ads, and children's ad requests do not include the advertising identifier.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency: on iOS, adults are asked for permission before the IDFA can be used for tracking. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared. Teens and children are never asked.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.

Google acts under its own privacy policy. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics (GameAnalytics)

To understand how the Games are played and to improve them (for example, where players get stuck, which levels are too hard, whether ads or purchases work correctly), the Games use GameAnalytics, provided by GameAnalytics ApS (Denmark).

  • When: only for players who are old enough to consent for themselves. Where consent is required (such as in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), analytics start only if you agree in the consent form, and you can change your choice later in the privacy options. Elsewhere, analytics are on by default and you can switch them off in the Game's settings. Children never send analytics.
  • What is sent: a random identifier created by the Game on your device (it is not linked to your name, account or advertising identifier), gameplay events (such as levels started and completed, in-game currency earned and spent, ads watched, purchases made, without any payment details), session start and length, error reports, the Game's version, and technical information such as device model, operating system version and language. GameAnalytics receives your IP address as part of the connection and uses it to determine your approximate country.
  • What is never sent: your age, name, email address, advertising identifier, precise location or anything you type.

Learn more: GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS) using your store account. We never receive your payment details, credit card number or billing address. The Game only receives the product you bought and a transaction identifier, which it uses to deliver the item, prevent double delivery and restore purchases. Purchase events may be included in analytics as described in section 4, without any payment details.

6. Cloud saves and sign-in

Some Games can store a copy of your progress in the cloud so you can continue on another device:

  • Android: Google Play Games Services (Saved Games). Sign-in is handled by Google, and your save is stored in your Google account.
  • iOS: Apple Game Center, with saves stored in your iCloud account. Sign-in is handled by Apple.

The Game receives only what is needed for this: your player profile's identifier and display name as provided by Google or Apple, and your save file. We do not receive your email address or password. Cloud saves are optional: the Games are fully playable without signing in. You can delete cloud saves through your Google Play Games or Game Center / iCloud settings. See Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

8. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent for personalized advertising, for the use of advertising identifiers and for analytics where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests for showing non-personalized ads that fund the Games, for ad fraud prevention, and for analytics where consent is not required (you can opt out at any time).
  • Performance of a contract for delivering in-app purchases and cloud saves you ask for.

9. International transfers and retention

Google may process data in the United States and other countries, using the safeguards described in its privacy policy (such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses). GameAnalytics may use service providers outside the EEA under similar safeguards. Data held by Google, Apple and GameAnalytics is kept according to their own retention policies. Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad and analytics consent at any time through the privacy options or settings in the Game.
  • Reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings; on iOS, change tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Uninstalling the Game deletes all locally stored data, including the random analytics identifier; a reinstall starts with a new one.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because we do not collect names or email addresses, we may not be able to link data to you; contact us and we will help as far as we can, including by forwarding deletion requests to GameAnalytics.

11. Children's privacy

The Games are not designed for the Apple App Store Kids category. Every Game asks for the player's age before any advertising or analytics service starts. For players identified as children we do not collect personal information: ads are child-directed and non-personalized without advertising identifiers, and analytics are not used. If you believe a child's personal information has been collected, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

12. Security

Data sent by the Games to the services above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date above.
