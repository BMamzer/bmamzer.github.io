Privacy Policy

Legends of Amberland mobile

Effective Date: 06.06.26

1. Information Collection and Use

We believe in absolute privacy. Because this game is entirely offline and does not require user accounts, cloud syncing, or online multiplayer features, it does not collect, access, store, or transmit any personal data or sensitive user information.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Personal identifiers (names, emails, phone numbers)
  • Device identifiers (IP addresses, IMEI numbers, Android IDs, or Apple IDFA)
  • Location data

Any gameplay data is stored strictly locally on your own device and is deleted if you uninstall the game.

2. Financial Data and Purchases

Your purchase transaction is processed entirely by the platform operator (Google Play for Android devices or Apple App Store for iOS devices) via your store account. The game itself does not collect, process, or have access to any of your financial data, billing details, or credit card information.

3. Third-Party Services

The game does not integrate any third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs), advertising networks, or analytics tools that track your behavior or collect data.

4. Children's Privacy

These services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children because we do not collect any data from any users whatsoever.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
