Powerlust

An old school action RPG roguelike with manual combat and a dark atmosphere.

Fight through dungeons, towns and a full story mode, collect legendary and set items, craft and combine gear, and build your hero your way. There's no auto combat: every hit and dodge is yours.

Free, offline, no pay-to-win

Powerlust is completely free and plays fully offline. There are no forced ads, only optional rewarded ads. Purchases are donations that come with cosmetic and quality-of-life rewards, like extra camera perspectives, character customization and a shared item stash.

Made by one developer

Powerlust is a passion project by Bartłomiej Mamzer, an independent developer who has been building and updating it for years.
